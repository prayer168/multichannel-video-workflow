# Channel brand presets

These are thumbnail composition suggestions. Apply the parent [Black Bear Science profile](../../profiles/blackbear-science.md) or [AI Watch profile](../../profiles/ai-watch.md) for brand decisions and use approved assets already available in the project or skill.

## 黑熊老師的自然教室
Audience: upper-primary pupils, families, teachers, science-curious adults.
Theme: real demonstrations, organisms, environment, inquiry, evidence.
Preferred hooks: wow, why, experiment, verification.
Visuals: authentic macro photo / slow-motion frame when available; one real phenomenon; restrained educational overlay; simple background; high natural contrast.
Text: Traditional Chinese, suggested 2–5 characters, meaningful question.
Hard guardrails: do not create unrealistic animal/plant physiology as supposedly documentary evidence; label illustrative generated scenes; distinguish measured result from hypothesis.
Avoid: cartoonish scientific falsehood, fake explosive effects, excessive arrows, multiple diagrams competing with the subject.

## AI Watch
Audience: educators, makers, knowledge workers, developers exploring AI workflows.
Theme: real results, actual UI (with privacy scrubbing), credible comparisons, time saving backed by data.
Preferred hooks: result, before/after, problem-solution, verification.
Visuals: one outcome or legible split comparison; restrained geometric treatment; avoid a clutter of brand logos; no unauthorized third-party marks.
Text: Traditional Chinese, suggested 2–5 characters, direct benefit or question.
Hard guardrails: claims about measured time, cost, speed must come from logged evidence; do not depict a polished product as shipped if it is a prototype.

## Shared
Maintain channel-specific type, safe contrast, flexible spacing, and a recognizable visual identity. Use existing approved assets or make a neutral draft if thumbnail-specific assets are unavailable. No fabricated headshot or personality likeness.
