# Production, accessibility, factual and licensing safeguards

All finished thumbnails, Shorts covers and Facebook share images are generated by Codex with Images 2.6 (see `codex-images-handoff.md`). Real footage is the reference input, not the deliverable.

## Source preference for references
1. User-owned frame from the final video that genuinely shows the subject or result.
2. User-provided licensed photograph or video.
3. No reference: a clearly conceptual image, recorded as `source_type: "illustration"` and never presented as recorded evidence.
Keep a source ledger for each reference frame and any brand asset, with approval/license status.

## Generation
- Images 2.6 renders the full image including the Traditional Chinese text. The prompt quotes the exact text; the output is checked character by character and regenerated on any mismatch (max 3 attempts).
- Keep the real subject, materials and observed result from the reference frame. Stylizing lighting, background or framing is fine; adding a result, number, person or product that the footage does not show is not.
- Subject occupies roughly 40–70% as a starting point, with clear space for the text.

## Inspection
Inspect full size and phone size (~160 px wide for 16:9, ~120 px wide for vertical) for legibility, clipping, focus, wrong glyphs, and mistaken claims.

## Resolution and metadata
Sizes and safe areas are in `codex-images-handoff.md`. YouTube and Facebook limits change—re-check https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431 before final upload. Final YouTube files are RGB JPEG quality ≥90; Facebook files stay PNG.

## Block conditions
- Missing authority to use a reference photo, footage or likeness: do not use it.
- Unverified spectacular science event or unmeasured speed/cost benefit: do not show it in image or text.
- Codex or Images 2.6 unavailable: deliver the brief with status `planned`. Do not substitute frame grabs or local renders as finished art.
- Never alter video-related evidence or secretly upload content.
